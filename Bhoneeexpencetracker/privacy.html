The short version

Bhonee Expense Tracker ("Bhonee") is made by App4We. Your money records stay on your phone. App4We has no servers that receive your data, there is no sign-up with us, and the app has no ads and no analytics or tracking tools. Data leaves your phone only when you choose a feature that needs it: a Google Drive backup, Poloshi AI with your own Claude API key, or a file you export and share.

What Bhonee stores on your phone

Transactions, accounts, categories, budgets, goals, notes, reminders, recurring payments, payments detected from notifications, Poloshi AI conversations, and bill photos or PDFs you attach. All of this is saved in the app's private storage on your phone. Other apps cannot read it.

Permissions and why they are used

  • Notifications: for the daily 9 PM reminder and the bill and card reminders you set.
  • Camera and photos: only when you choose to scan a receipt or attach a bill. The receipt text is read on your phone using Google ML Kit's on-device text recognition.
  • Fingerprint, face or PIN (App Lock): handled by Android. Bhonee only learns whether unlocking succeeded, never your fingerprint or PIN.
  • Notification access (optional, Auto-detect payments): if you switch this on, Bhonee looks at incoming notifications from bank SMS and payment apps on your phone and keeps only the ones that look like payments (an amount plus words like "debited" or "paid"). OTPs and offers are skipped. They are shown to you to add or ignore and are never uploaded. You can turn this off at any time in Android's notification access settings.
  • Internet: for Google Drive backup, Poloshi AI with Claude, opening these web pages, and downloading the app's typeface from Google Fonts.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you turn on backup, you sign in with your Google account and Bhonee saves a copy of your data and attachments in the hidden app-data folder of your own Google Drive. Only Bhonee can read that folder; App4We cannot access it. Google's Privacy Policy applies to data stored in Google Drive. You can delete these backups from Google Drive (Settings → Manage apps) at any time.

Poloshi AI

Without an API key, Poloshi AI analyses your data on your phone.

If you add your own Claude API key, each question you ask is sent to Anthropic, together with a summary of your money so Claude can answer: account balances, monthly totals by category, budgets, goals, recurring payments, and your last 90 days of transactions including their notes. Bill photos, notes from the Notes section and SMS are not sent. This is processed by Anthropic under your own Anthropic account and Anthropic's Privacy Policy. Your API key is stored only on your phone and is never included in exports or backups. Remove the key in Settings → Poloshi AI to stop sending data.

Import and export

When you export, Bhonee creates a file (Excel/CSV, Bhonee backup file or PDF report) and hands it to the app you choose. Where it goes from there is up to you. Imported files are read on your phone.

Your choices

  • Delete everything in the app with Settings → Delete all data, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Delete Google Drive backups from your Google Drive settings.
  • Delete Poloshi AI conversations from the chat's History.
  • Turn off notification access, backup or Poloshi AI with Claude at any time.

Children

Bhonee is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
