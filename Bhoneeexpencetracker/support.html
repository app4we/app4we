Getting started

How do I add an expense quickly?

Tap the blue + button, type the amount on Bhonee's keypad (you can do sums like 120+80), choose the category, and save. Start typing a note and Bhonee suggests past entries to fill everything in one tap. You can also add from the home-screen widget.

How do I move my data from Money Manager?

Export your records from Money Manager as an Excel/CSV file, then in Bhonee go to Settings → Import & Export → Import and pick the file. Categories, accounts and transfers are matched automatically.

How do I add the home-screen widget?

Long-press an empty spot on your home screen, tap Widgets, find Bhonee and drag it to your home screen. It shows today's and this month's spending, with a + to add an expense.

Backup & your data

How do I back up my data?

Go to Settings → Backup & Restore and sign in with Google. Bhonee backs up to your own Google Drive after changes and every night, including bill photos and PDFs. You can also export a file from Settings → Import & Export.

How do I restore on a new phone?

Install Bhonee, open Settings → Backup & Restore, sign in with the same Google account and tap Restore.

Where is my data stored?

On your phone, and in your own Google Drive if you turn on backup. See the Privacy Policy.

Features

Auto-detect payments isn't picking up a payment
  • Check that Bhonee is allowed in Android's notification access (Settings → Auto-detect payments → Turn on).
  • Bhonee can only see payments that show a notification. Some apps don't show one after you pay inside them, and some banks don't send an SMS for small UPI payments. Add those by hand.
  • Make sure your SMS app and payment apps are allowed to show notifications.
  • If you already added the same amount at about the same time, Bhonee skips it to avoid duplicates.
My credit card bill in Bhonee doesn't match the bank's statement

Bhonee estimates each bill from the card expenses you entered in that cycle. If the bank posted a swipe on a different day, the totals differ. Open the card, tap Enter statement amount on the last bill and type the amount from your statement. Also check the statement and due dates with the ✎ button.

What does Needs / Wants / Savings mean?

It's the 50/30/20 guide: about 50% of income on needs, up to 30% on wants, and at least 20% to savings. Each category belongs to a group, which you can change in Settings → Categories. Tap the ⓘ on the card for your own numbers.

How do I use Poloshi AI with Claude?

Create an API key in the Anthropic Console (console.anthropic.com → API Keys; it needs prepaid credit), then paste it in Settings → Poloshi AI and tap Test it. Anthropic charges your account for each question. Without a key, Poloshi still answers from your data on the phone.

I'm not getting the 9 PM reminder

Allow notifications for Bhonee in Android Settings → Apps → Bhonee → Notifications, and turn off battery restrictions for Bhonee if your phone has them.
